STEP-BY-STEP ARTIST GUIDE

How to Paint & Care

Take your time! You don't have to finish in one day. Enjoy and have fun.

🖌️ How to Paint

  • Place the canvas on a flat surface.
  • Start by painting the small and narrow areas first, using the tip of the brush.
  • Close the paint pot tightly after use to prevent dryness.
  • Rinse your brush in water between colors and wipe it on a tissue until no color remains.
  • Right-handed: paint from left to right. Left-handed: paint from right to left to prevent smudging.
  • Made a mistake? Don't worry! Let the paint dry completely, then paint over it with the correct color.
  • If the paint is too thick or dried up, add a drop or two of water and mix well.

✨ Tips & Care

  • Keep your brushes clean after every session.
  • Work in a well-lit area for the best view of numbers.
  • Protect your clothes and workspace before starting.
  • Save extra paint for final touchups.
  • Acrylic paints dry quickly — keep lids closed when not in use.
  • Use the QR code inside your kit to zoom in and see small numbers clearly.
